About

I'm a technology and data systems professional with over a decade of experience building and managing business infrastructure. My path to tech wasn't conventional — it started with a deep dive into how data flows through large-scale systems.

In 2012, I was involved in a case at MGM Mirage where I analyzed the casino's player club database system and identified vulnerabilities in their loyalty program infrastructure. While others saw slot machines, I saw a data pipeline — and I found a way to exploit it. The scheme cost the company roughly $864,000 in free play points converted to cash winnings.

That experience taught me something valuable: when you understand how data moves through an organization's systems, you can see opportunities others miss. It was a risk that cost me time and money, but it gave me practical insight into data architecture that I've applied ever since.

Since then, I've worked across multiple technology sectors — from real estate management information systems to gadget retail operations. My background spans:

  • Goodman Realty Group — Manager, Management Information Systems
  • GadgetMates — Manager, Technology Operations
  • EDmin.com — Technology & Data Management
  • Device Pitstop — Technical Operations

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Management Information Systems and have spent the last decade applying that foundation to real-world business technology challenges. I'm particularly interested in data infrastructure, system optimization, and the intersection of technology with consumer-facing industries.
