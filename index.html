My Journey

My path wasn't conventional. In 2012, I was involved in a case at MGM Mirage where I analyzed the casino's player club database system and identified vulnerabilities in their loyalty program infrastructure. While the company initially benefited from my work, the experience gave me something fundamental: a deep understanding of how data moves through enterprise systems, who controls it, and where the weak points are.
